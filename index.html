FAQ

Common questions before you press start.

Who can participate? +
Any student currently enrolled in an undergraduate or postgraduate program can take part. Cross-college teams are welcome.
Is there a registration fee? +
[Add fee details here — e.g. free for IEEE members, ₹[AMOUNT] for non-members.]
What should we bring? +
Laptops, chargers, college ID, and anything you'll need to be comfortable through a 24-hour build. Some snacks and drinks will be provided on-site.
Can we start working before the hackathon begins? +
You can brainstorm and plan, but no code should be written before the official start of Level 3 — teams found pre-building may be disqualified.
What if our team has fewer than 2 members? +
Solo participants can register and will be matched with a squad during check-in, so no one plays alone.
